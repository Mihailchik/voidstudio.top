Argyns

A digital registry for the Argyn clans.

Website ↗︎App Store ↗︎Google Play ↗︎

What it is

Argyns is a digital registry for the Argyn clans. The app brings descendants together and helps keep the history of the clan. It is available on the App Store and Google Play.

How it works

01

A shared database

One database with up-to-date statistics on clan members.

02

Clan counter

The size of every clan is shown at a glance.

03

Notifications

Important news and gathering announcements arrive as notifications.

04

Sign-up with a city

People give their city when they register.

05

iOS and Android

One Flutter codebase runs on both platforms.
