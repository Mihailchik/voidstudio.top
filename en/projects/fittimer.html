FitTimer

An interval timer that lets you keep your eyes off the phone.

App Store ↗Code on GitHub ↗

What it is

FitTimer is an interval training timer for iPhone and iPad: Tabata, HIIT, EMOM, AMRAP and your own circuits. The app is available on the App Store.

How it works

01

Quick start

Three wheels: work, rest, rounds. A workout starts in a few seconds.

02

Templates

Tabata, EMOM, AMRAP and HIIT can be started as they are or saved and edited.

03

Your workouts

A workout is built from blocks with repeats: warm-up, circuit, cool-down. Drag blocks to reorder them.

04

Workout screen

The whole screen takes the colour of the phase. Big digits, a progress ring and a hint about what comes next.

05

Cues

The 3-2-1 countdown plays over your music. If the screen is off, interval changes arrive as notifications.

06

Exact time

Time is counted from the device clock, so it stays right after pauses and switching apps.

07

No account

No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Data stays on the device.

Built with: Flutter · Dart · iPhone and iPad
