What we have built.

Interactive graphics, a surf forecast, a mobile app, a bot and a publishing system.

01

SurfWeb service

A site for surfers: will there be waves today.

Working this out normally means opening a few weather sites and adding them up in your head. Here it is already done: for every beach you see the wave height, the wind, the tide and live cameras. The forecast is pulled from several weather services, so one of them being wrong does not send you to a flat sea. Phuket came first and more spots keep being added.

Vanilla JS · Open-Meteo · MET Norway · NOAA

02

InkfallInteractive graphic

A browser toy: you paint with ink on water.

Move the mouse or a finger and the colour spreads the way real ink does. Turn the camera on and paint with your hand in the air, or turn the microphone on and steer it with your voice. A good frame saves as a picture and the whole session records as video. Nothing to install, it opens from a link.

WebGL2 · GLSL · MediaPipe · Web Audio · single HTML file

05

Podcast BotTelegram bot

Every podcast lands in one Telegram channel.

The bot watches websites and YouTube for new episodes, downloads them and sends them to the channel as a plain audio file. Long episodes are split so they fit the size limit. For anyone who listens fast, a sped-up version arrives next to the original.

Python · aiogram · yt-dlp · ffmpeg · SQLite

06

FitTimerMobile app

A workout timer you never have to look at.

Set how long to work, how long to rest and how many rounds, hit start and put the phone away. Three seconds before every switch it counts down out loud, and you hear it over your music and with the screen off. Ready-made modes like tabata are built in, and you can put together your own. Free, with no ads and no sign-up.

Flutter · Dart · iPhone and iPad

07

EyeballBrowser game

How good is your eye? Five tasks a day.

Stop a timer at five seconds without looking, cut a line in half, set an angle, find the centre of a circle, guess how many dots there are. No ruler allowed. Everyone gets the same set and it changes daily, so scores are worth comparing with friends. A full run takes under a minute.

Vanilla JS · SVG · localStorage · no server
