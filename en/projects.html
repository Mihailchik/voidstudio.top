InkfallInteractive graphic

Ink in water: mouse, finger, hand or voice.

A GPU fluid simulation with six ink behaviours. It follows the cursor and several fingers at once, hand gestures in front of the camera, and voice or music from the microphone. The camera image stays on the device and is never uploaded. Export PNG stills or record video up to 4K.
