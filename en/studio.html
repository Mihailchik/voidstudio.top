04

FitTimer

An interval timer: three fields for a quick start, or a routine of your own.

Set work, rest and rounds and press start, with Tabata, EMOM, AMRAP and HIIT presets one tap away. Your own workouts are built from blocks of intervals and saved, and history keeps every finished session. A circular timer shows what comes next, and a three-second countdown before each switch is heard over music and with the screen locked.
